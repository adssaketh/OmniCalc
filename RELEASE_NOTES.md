# OmniCalc 1.2.0

OmniCalc has been rebuilt from the ground up with the same look, and it now includes the full function set of the Casio fx-991ES PLUS (2nd edition) inside the calculator and its Advanced keys.

## Highlights

- **Natural display.** Type fractions, powers, subscripts, square roots, integrals, sums and products the way they are written on paper. Results show as exact fractions, √ and π forms, with one tap to switch to decimals.
- **Casio calculation engine.** Every function of the fx-991ES PLUS: ∫, d/dx, Σ, ∏, SOLVE and CALC, the 40 scientific constants, the 40 unit conversions, and the modes COMP, CMPLX, STAT, BASE-N, EQN, MATRIX, VECTOR, TABLE, INEQ, VERIF and DIST. SETUP covers angle unit, number format, fraction format, complex format, decimal point and contrast.
- **Two calculation styles.** Choose Casio (default) or OmniCalc (the previous behaviour) in Settings. Percent keeps the phone-style rules by default (`100+10%` is 110).
- **Chemistry.** A periodic table of all 118 elements, a formula parser with molar mass and mass percent, an equation balancer and solvers for gases, solutions, acids and bases, thermochemistry, kinetics, equilibrium and more. Chemistry is off by default; turn it on in Settings.
- **Everything else carries over.** The converter, currency, finance, physics and grapher screens, all themes (light, dark and dynamic) and the liquid-glass bars.

## Good to know

- Statistics, matrix, vector and table editors open as full-height sheets, and INEQ, EQN and DIST open as dialogs, instead of copying the Casio screens line for line.
- A few behaviours could not be checked against a physical fx-991ES and are listed in the project notes as unverified.
- This build is signed and uses the same app id as earlier OmniCalc versions.
