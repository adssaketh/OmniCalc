Privacy Policy

OmniCalc • Effective Date: October 2026
100% On-Device & Zero Data Collection

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

OmniCalc ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by Saketh with a strict privacy-first, offline-first architecture. We believe your calculations, finances, formulas, and scientific work belong exclusively to you.

OmniCalc does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage metrics, or calculation history with anyone.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app operates entirely on your device. The following data is stored locally in your device's browser/app storage (HTML5 LocalStorage) solely to provide uninterrupted usability:

  • Calculation History & Recents: Previous arithmetic and scientific calculations entered by you so you can revisit them.
  • User Preferences: Your selected Material 3 Expressive theme, angle mode (Degrees vs. Radians), decimal precision, audio feedback toggle, and grapher visibility.
  • SIP Financial Variables: Current parameters entered in the financial planner (monthly investment, expected rate of return, tenure, annual step-up).

This information never leaves your device. You can erase all stored data at any time by clicking "Clear All" in the Recents panel or clearing app storage in your Android settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

OmniCalc contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking cookies, and no third-party telemetry tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics). No tracking identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Android ID) are gathered or shared.

4. Device Permissions & Audio

  • No Network Permissions for Personal Data: Network access is solely used by the browser/PWA container to download static application assets and service worker caches for offline operation.
  • Web Audio API: Sound feedback utilizes procedural Web Audio synthesized oscillators locally on your device. The app does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, or location.
  • Haptic Feedback: The app utilizes the standard browser vibration API for optional tactile keypress feedback.

5. Children’s Privacy & Google Play Families Compliance

OmniCalc is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Developer Policy on Children and Families. Because we collect zero personal data from any user regardless of age, the application is completely safe for children, students, teachers, and professionals alike.

6. Security & Data Protection

Because all processing occurs directly within your device's sandboxed JavaScript runtime, your numbers and financial projections are protected by your operating system’s security boundaries. We maintain no external databases containing your entries.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page and documented in the in-app settings. Continued use of OmniCalc constitutes acceptance of the terms.
